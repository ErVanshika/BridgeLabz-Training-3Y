Welcome to Mathura

Mathura is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is known for its rich cultural heritage, temples, traditions, and historical importance. The city is situated on the banks of the Yamuna River.

History of Mathura

Mathura has a long and significant history and is associated with ancient Indian culture and traditions. The city has been an important center of art, religion, and trade throughout different periods of history.

Popular Places

Mathura has many places that attract visitors and showcase its cultural heritage.

  • Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi
  • Dwarkadhish Temple
  • Vishram Ghat
  • Mathura Museum

Culture and Traditions

Mathura is known for festivals, traditional music, dance, local food, handicrafts, and religious traditions. Festivals such as Holi are celebrated with various cultural activities throughout the region.
